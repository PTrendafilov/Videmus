The Problem

Drivers need guidance.
We provide it.

Every day, drivers face moments of uncertainty on the road — a tight parking space, a pedestrian stepping onto a crossing, a light turning green at a busy intersection. Inexperienced drivers feel exposed in these moments. Experienced ones make mistakes. Videmus addresses this through real-time AR guidance powered by Computer Vision, helping drivers make the right decision at the right moment — and reducing the crashes that result from hesitation and human error.

How It Works

AR guidance for every moment
on the road

Scenario 01

Parking

Parking in tight or unfamiliar spaces is one of the most stressful moments for any driver. Videmus projects an AR arrow directly onto your field of view, guiding the car into the space with precision. No more guessing. No more scrapes.

Scenario 02

Pedestrian Crossing

A pedestrian stepping onto the road can appear in seconds. Videmus detects the crossing in real time, highlights the person with a warning overlay, and instructs the driver to stop — before the situation becomes dangerous.

Scenario 03

Traffic Navigation

Intersections demand quick, accurate decisions. Videmus reads the traffic light, prepares the driver for the change, and guides the turn with a clear AR path overlay — keeping the driver confident and the road safe.
